Ultimate Chocolate Chip Cookie Recipe

Ingredients

  • 285g Flour
  • 70g Regular Sugar
  • 140g Brown Sugar
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 Extra Egg Yolk, Chilled
  • 200g Butter
  • 1/4 tsp Baking Soda
  • 1/2 tsp Baking Powder
  • 1 tsp Corn Starch
  • Vanilla Extract to Taste
  • Chocolate to Taste
  • A Pinch of Salt

Method

  1. Brown the butter. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat, stirring until it turns golden brown and smells nutty. Let it cool slightly.
  2. Mix the sugars. Add the regular sugar and brown sugar to the butter. Mix until the mixture becomes lighter in colour.
  3. Add an ice cube. Carefully mix in one small ice cube to replace some of the water lost while browning the butter. Let it melt before continuing.
  4. Add the eggs. Mix in the egg and chilled egg yolk until combined. Add vanilla extract to taste.
  5. Mix the dry ingredients. Add the flour, baking soda, baking powder, corn starch and salt. Mix until a dough forms, without overmixing.
  6. Add the chocolate. Chop the chocolate into pieces and fold it into the cookie dough.
  7. Bake the cookies. Shape the dough into portions and place them on a lined baking tray with space between each cookie. Bake at 175°C until the edges are golden and the centres are just set. Check them regularly.
  8. Cool and enjoy. Leave the cookies on the tray for a few minutes before transferring them to a cooling rack.
