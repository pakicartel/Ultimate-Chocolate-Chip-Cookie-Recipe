Ultimate Chocolate Chip Cookie Recipe

Ingredients

  • 285g flour
  • 70g regular sugar
  • 140g brown sugar
  • 1 egg
  • 1 extra egg yolk, chilled
  • 200g butter
  • 1/4 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tsp corn starch
  • Vanilla extract to taste
  • Chocolate to taste
  • A pinch of salt
